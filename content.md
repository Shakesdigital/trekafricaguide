## About this activity
Chimpanzees are the signature primate of Nyungwe, and [Nyungwe National Park](/attractions/nyungwe-national-park) is their most accessible Rwandan stronghold. Visit Rwanda describes a small, mobile population in one of Africa's oldest rainforests, tracked at dawn rather than on a fixed circuit. The payoff is a forest walk at altitude in cool mountain air, ending — if the forest allows — with an hour observing a habituated community. Read our [Nyungwe National Park](/attractions/nyungwe-national-park) guide for the wider park context including the [canopy walkway](/activities/nyungwe-canopy-walk), the other signature experience.

### How the morning usually runs
Treat this as a full-morning outing, not an hour's stroll. Visit Rwanda specifies a 05:30 gathering at the Uwinka Reception Centre, where briefing and group allocation happen before the walk into the forest. Permits are sold first come, first served and times are allocated once you are booked, so a last-minute arrival is a risk rather than a plan. Once in the forest, rangers follow the community's calls and nesting signs; the walk can take from an hour to several hours over steep, maintained footpaths that turn muddy after rain. Viewing happens once the chimpanzees are located, limited to one hour at a minimum distance of ten metres, with no flash photography, no eating or drinking on the trail, and no mimicking calls. After the encounter, groups return to Uwinka; operators running a Kigali day trip advertise roughly twelve hours door to door, with about five hours of driving each way.

### Permits, fees and booking
A separate chimpanzee tracking ticket is required — a canopy walk ticket does not cover it, and neither works without its own confirmation. Tickets are bought through RDB's online booking portal, an RDB office, or a registered tour operator. RDB's published fee table lists USD 150 for an adult foreigner; a lower resident rate (USD 75) applies to foreign residents of Rwanda after four consecutive months' documented residency, with similar resident rates for East African citizens and residents, and lower local rates for Rwandan citizens. Residents need passport or work-permit evidence in hand. RDB verifies documents at the park entry, and warns that erroneous or false applications lose entry even after payment. Cash is not accepted at the park; book through the official channel and carry matching documents.

### Pairing it with the canopy walk
Because both activities depart from Uwinka and the canopy walkway's bridge is reached via the same Igishigishigi trail network, a two-day Nyungwe stay lets you pair the dawn chimp walk one day with the canopy walk the next — the sequence the park's own guidance recommends. Stacking both into one morning is possible but exhausting; the chimp walk's variable return time makes a fixed canopy slot risky. Our [Nyungwe canopy walk guide](/activities/nyungwe-canopy-walk) covers the bridge outing and its separate ticket.

### A Kigali day-trip option
Travellers basing in the capital can cover the long road day in one booking. The exact listing we verified — "Kigali: 1-Day Nyungwe Chimpanzee Tracking & Canopy Walk Tour" from operator Vista Safaris — is a one-day return with Kigali accommodation pickup, guides in English or French, advertised from €320 per person at our check, free cancellation up to 24 hours, and a reserve-now-pay-later option. It bundles the chimp permit, canopy ticket, and park fees. Read the inclusions line by line before paying: the listing describes park fees as an additional charge, and its own tour copy may describe trail conditions that differ from current park guidance.

## Best time to participate
June to mid-September is generally drier, although rain remains possible year-round in montane forest. Rwanda's rainy periods typically run from mid-February to May and mid-September to mid-December. Forest walks can be muddy in any season, so choose dates around confirmed permits and prepare for changing conditions.

## What to bring
- Waterproof hiking boots with good grip
- Light waterproof jacket
- Long trousers and long sleeves (biting insects are common)
- Gloves for gripping vegetation
- Small daypack with water and snacks
- Camera (no flash permitted during viewing)
- Permit confirmation and matching ID documents

## Suitability and traveller perspective
The official minimum age is 15. Discuss fitness and health concerns before buying a permit; the walk involves steep gradients at altitude (Uwinka sits above 2,400 m) and the pace follows the chimpanzees' movements. RDB instructs visitors with a cold, flu or another contagious illness not to visit the chimpanzees. Ask the organiser how to handle illness and permit changes before departure. In a TripAdvisor review dated August 2026, a UK visitor praised the ranger's knowledge of Nyungwe's birdlife along the trail but warned that the 5:30 a.m. start from Kigali meant a 2:30 a.m. hotel departure — a useful reminder to consider the whole travel day, not just the forest walk.
