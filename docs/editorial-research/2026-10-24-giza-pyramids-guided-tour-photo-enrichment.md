## Guide fit and method

Read the full published activity guide before selection. It describes outdoor guiding on the Giza Plateau around the pyramids and Sphinx, an optional separately ticketed pyramid interior, and a possible second museum segment at the Grand Egyptian Museum with timed entry. The six images below document those two exact guide locations and different useful views: monuments at the plateau, visitor scale, the museum-to-pyramid view, museum exterior, and a close Sphinx view. They are illustrations of the places only; they do not show or imply this tour's operator or participants, nor establish a particular tour sequence.
