## Primary Sources Checked (2026-11-21)

### 1. UK FCDO Algeria Travel Advice
- **URL:** https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/algeria
- **Checked:** 2026-11-21
- **Key facts:**
  - FCDO advises against all travel to areas within 30km of borders with Libya, Niger, Mali, Mauritania, and Western Sahara (includes Tindouf, Illizi, Djanet areas)
  - FCDO advises against all but essential travel to the rest of the Sahara (including Tassili n'Ajjer)
  - Northern coastal areas (Algiers, Oran, Constantine) have different advisory level
  - Entry requirements: passport valid 6 months, visa required for most nationalities, apply via Algerian embassy/consulate
  - No e-visa system for tourism; visa must be obtained before travel
  - Yellow fever certificate required if arriving from risk country
  - Currency: Algerian dinar (DZD), closed currency, declare on entry/exit
  - Local laws: photography restrictions near military/government sites, dress modestly, Ramadan observance

### 2. Smartraveller (Australia) Algeria Advice
- **URL:** https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/africa/algeria
- **Checked:** 2026-11-21
- **Key facts:**
  - "Do not travel" to southern provinces including Illizi (Djanet/Tassili)
  - "Reconsider your need to travel" to remaining Saharan areas
  - "Exercise a high degree of caution" in northern coastal cities
  - Terrorism and kidnapping risk in south and east
  - Border areas with Libya, Niger, Mali, Mauritania, Western Sahara: high risk

### 3. UNESCO World Heritage Centre - Tassili n'Ajjer
- **URL:** https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/179
- **Checked:** 2026-11-21
- **Key facts:**
  - Inscribed 1982, criteria (i)(iii)(vii)(viii)
  - 72,000 km² plateau, exceptional rock art (15,000+ engravings/paintings, 6000 BC - early centuries AD)
  - Geological formations: sandstone forests, arches, canyons
  - Cultural significance: Tuareg people, prehistoric climate change evidence
  - Administered by Office National de Gestion et d'Exploitation des Aires Protégées (ONGEAP)
  - Access requires authorized guide and permit

### 4. Algerian Visa / Entry Requirements
- **Official visa portal:** No unified e-visa for tourism; applications through Algerian diplomatic missions
- **Visa types:** Tourist visa (single/multiple entry), business visa, transit visa
- **Required documents:** Passport (6 months validity, 2 blank pages), application form, photos, invitation letter or hotel booking confirmation, travel insurance, flight itinerary
- **Processing time:** 10-20 working days typically
- **Cost:** Varies by nationality and mission (~€80-120 for single entry)
- **Yellow fever:** Certificate required if arriving from endemic country (WHO list)

### 5. Djanet Access and Logistics
- **Airport:** Djanet Inedbirene Airport (DJG), domestic flights from Algiers (Air Algérie, ~2h 30m)
- **Flight frequency:** 2-3 weekly flights, seasonal variations, subject to cancellation
- **Ground transport:** 4x4 essential for Tassili; no public transport to rock art sites
- **Permits:** Tassili n'Ajjer National Park permit required (obtained through authorized tour operator/guide)
- **Guide requirement:** Mandatory authorized local guide for all Tassili visits (ONGEAP regulation)
- **Seasonality:** 
  - Oct-Apr: milder daytime temps (20-30°C), cold nights (0-10°C), best for walking
  - May-Sep: extreme heat (40-50°C+), not recommended for extended desert travel
  - Wind: Harmattan (Nov-Mar) brings dust/sand storms
