## Scope and selection

All six files were visually inspected. Their original Commons SHA-1 values and exact file-page URLs were compared against the 145 Commons source records in the existing image library; local SHA-256 values were compared against all recorded local hashes. No source URL or image hash is reused. Existing Cape Town destination gallery sources (Company’s Garden, Bo-Kaap, Camps Bay, V&A Waterfront, Sea Point coast and City Bowl) were excluded.
