# Maasai Mara attraction photo enrichment — 22 October 2026

**Page:** https://trekafricaguide.com/attractions/maasai-mara/  
**Before:** published attraction page with two generic/default images.  
**After:** six visually inspected, distinct Commons photographs illustrating the reserve landscape and wildlife. The prior generic acacia default and hot-air-balloon starter image were removed from this detail-page gallery; the balloon image was not supported by the reviewed guide and is not reused. No factual guide text, relations, tracking, or layout changed.

The guide describes a broad ecosystem and distinguishes the reserve from neighboring conservancies. It cautions against promising migration or a river crossing on a fixed date. Captions below state image dates and do not promise present wildlife sightings, camp location, access, or itinerary. The listed image sizes are Wikimedia 1920-pixel thumbnails; no other image edits were made. All sources were checked 22 October 2026. Source URL and SHA-256 comparisons against every other photo-library assignment and local public image found no duplicate.

The existing renderer shows five lead photos and a “See all 6” lightbox; no renderer changes were needed. This is a dated visual collection, not evidence of the current season, animal movements, visitor access, or a camp's location.
