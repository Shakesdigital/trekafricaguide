## Claims checked on 17 October

- UWA's [current Kibale visitor page](https://ugandawildlife.org/national-parks/kibale-national-park/) describes the Kanyanchu Visitor Centre, chimpanzee tracking and habituation, indicative departure times, duration, group size and minimum age. A separate UWA [Kibale page](https://ugandawildlife.org/national_parks/kibale/) says tracking and habituation occur year-round and notes muddy wet-season trails and chimpanzees ranging deeper in the forest during drier periods.
- UWA's [July 2024 chimpanzee and gorilla tracking guidelines](https://ugandawildlife.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Guidelines-for-the-management-of-gorilla-and-chimpanzee-tracking-JULY-2024.pdf) require permit validation, briefing and accompaniment by UWA guides. Search-extracted guidance says standard chimpanzee tracking begins at 8:00 a.m. or 2:00 p.m. Departure times and age requirements differ between UWA pages/materials, and the linked tariff covered July 2024–June 2026; the public guide therefore directs travelers to confirm current terms rather than publishing a fixed timetable, age limit or price.
- UWA's [Kibale park page](https://ugandawildlife.org/national-parks/kibale-national-park/) gives route guidance from Kampala via Fort Portal and alternative flight/road connections. The guide presents travel time as an estimate and asks travelers to confirm current roads and final local transfers.
- A [Tripadvisor review page for Kibale Forest National Park](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g608446-d6199605-Reviews-Kibale_Forest_National_Park-Fort_Portal_Western_Region.html) displayed a visitor review dated January 2026 praising the guide's interpretation and warning about biting ants. This is explicitly framed as one reviewer's experience, not a general guarantee.

## Remaining uncertainty

UWA source pages disagree on departure times and minimum age; the 2024 operational guidelines and fee tariff may have been superseded. Confirm permit, eligibility, schedule, fee and reporting point with UWA or a licensed operator before booking. Weather, trail difficulty, chimpanzee movements and sightings vary. The guide makes no sighting promise.
