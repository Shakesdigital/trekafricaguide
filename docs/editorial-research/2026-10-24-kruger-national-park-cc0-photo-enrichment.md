# Kruger National Park: no-attribution photo enrichment — 24 October 2026

## Scope and review

Reviewed the published Kruger National Park guide and its existing image assignments. The guide emphasizes self-drive and guided safari choices, varied habitats, wildlife and birdlife, and planning across the park’s scale. The live gallery had two photographs: the existing Kruger elephant hero (CC BY-SA) and existing safari-road scene (CC0). Both remain in the gallery. Seven distinct, visually inspected photographs were added; every new image is explicitly CC0 or released into the public domain. No existing required-attribution photo was changed or removed, and no public attribution is required for the added files.

Each selected file was visually inspected. Source pages and versions were checked on 24 October 2026. Commons thumbnails were saved as supplied; no local crop or other change was made. Source-page URLs and SHA-256 hashes were compared against the full current library, all other public image assets and the seven-image selection; no duplicates were found. CC0/public-domain permissions carry no public attribution obligation. Creator, source page, revision, licence, capture/check date, evidence, alt text, rights note and local hashes are preserved in the internal photo library.

## Exclusions and preservation

Three lower-quality early candidate images were excluded after visual review. No guide prose or captions were changed. The two existing gallery assets were kept in the assignment; the existing CC BY-SA hero and all of its library metadata remain unchanged. No layout, relationships, booking targets, analytics, Stay22 IDs, redirects or canonicals were changed.
