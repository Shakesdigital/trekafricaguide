## Claim-level sources (all checked 9 November 2026)

1. Visit Rwanda (RDB tourism board) gorilla tracking page — Volcanoes facts: 12 fully habituated gorilla families; 8 permits per troop per day, 96 permits per day; briefing at Kinigi headquarters 07:00 with groups allocated on the day by fitness; hikes 30 minutes to 4+ hours reaching 2,500 m–4,000 m; one-hour daily contact per group; ~1,000 wild mountain gorillas with 604 in the Virunga Massif; **gorilla permit cost US$1,500 per person per day; minimum age 15**; special offers for Rwandan and EAC residents; 10% of permit revenue channelled to communities; booking by advance through the **Irembo platform** or a licensed operator. https://visitrwanda.com/interests/gorilla-tracking/
2. UWA official Bwindi park page — Bwindi facts: ~459 mountain gorillas ("almost half" of the world population); 25 habituated groups, nine for tourism and one for research; Mubare first habituated for tourism April 1993; park altitude 1,160–2,607 m; size 321 km²; park entry FNR US$40 / FR US$30 / EAC UGX 20,000; named road routes (Kampala–Ntungamo–Rukungiri–Kihihi–Buhoma ≈390 km tarmac + 82 km murram; Kampala–Kabale onward routes to Ruhija 95 km ≈4 h and Nkuringo 105 km ≈4 h; Kabale–Kisoro 80 km); daily flights Entebbe→Kisoro (southern sectors) and Entebbe→Kihihi (north), ≈1 h 10 min; 4WD repeatedly recommended. https://ugandawildlife.org/national-parks/bwindi-impenetrable-national-park/
3. UWA Conservation Tariff PDF (uploaded 2024, titled "Conservation Tariff 2024 – 2026", currently linked as "UWA RATES" from ugandawildlife.org) — **GORILLA TRACKING IN BINP AND MGNP: Gorilla Permit FNR US$800 / ROA US$500 / FR US$700 / EAC UGX 300,000; Gorilla Habituation 1,500/1,000/1,000/750,000; rates include guide fee, park entrance fees for the day and community development contribution**; gorilla tracking rules (min age 15, max 8 visitors per group, 10 m distance, one hour per group per day). Validity caveat: the document states a period ending June 2026; figures were cross-checked against the fee table the UWA site still publishes and must be re-confirmed with UWA at booking time. https://ugandawildlife.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/UWA-Conservation-Tariff-July-2024-June-2026.pdf
4. Rwanda Directorate General of Immigration and Emigration (migration.gov.rw) via official-page text — since 1 January 2018 citizens of all countries receive visa on arrival at ports and land borders; single-entry visitor visa fee not exceeding US$50, multiple-entry US$70; 30-day fee waiver for citizens of AU/Commonwealth/La Francophonie member states; EAC citizens free up to six months. https://www.migration.gov.rw/visa-on-arrival

East Africa Tourist Visa (RwandAir AIP/information text served by Rwanda authority channels) — EATV costs US$100, valid 90 days, multiple entry between Rwanda, Kenya and Uganda; when applied for before travel, first entry must be through the issuing country.
