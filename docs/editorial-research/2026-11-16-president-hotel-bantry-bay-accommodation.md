## Scope

New accommodation guide: President Hotel, 4 Alexander Road, Bantry Bay (Sea Point), Cape Town 8001.
Full-service Atlantic Seaboard hotel: Classic Rooms (~31 sqm, ocean/mountain/urban views, semi-catering
kitchenettes), Suites (~49 sqm, living areas, Ocean Suites up to four adults), Apartments (~49 sqm,
families/long stays) and a Presidential two-bedroom apartment. Five on-site venues (Restaurant & Terrace,
Botany Café, The Senate, The Base, The Deck), Cove Wellness Spa, heated infinity pool with pool bar,
Green Key certification. Unusually documented inclusion provision: accessible rooms, Deafgard devices,
guide dogs welcome, kosher-experienced kitchen with Sabbath-open stairwells, Crescent Rating 5/7
Muslim-friendly services, Autism SA neurodiversity-trained staff. Location: Saunders' Rock tidal pool
~100 m, Queen's Beach ~150 m, MyCiTi Queens Beach station ~250 m, V&A Waterfront ~10 min, airport ~25 min
(operator) / 24 km (Booking.com). Check-in from 14:00, check-out by 11:00. Cross-links: Cape Town
destination, Cape Town attraction, Table Mountain guided hike, Mount Nelson (City Bowl contrast), the
Cape Town base-by-outings insight, South Africa country page.

## Operator-listed rates (checked 16 November 2026, reconfirm before booking)
