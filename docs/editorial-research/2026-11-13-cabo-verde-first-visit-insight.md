## Official Sources (all checked 2026-11-13)

### 1. Instituto do Turismo de Cabo Verde — Official Tourism Portal
- **URL**: https://www.visit-caboverde.com/en/
- **Islands overview**: https://www.visit-caboverde.com/en/islands
- **Travel Wise (practical info)**: https://www.visit-caboverde.com/en/travel-wise
- **Key facts retrieved**:
  - 10 islands, 9 inhabited (Santa Luzia uninhabited, restricted access)
  - 4 international airports: Praia (Santiago, Nelson Mandela), Sal (Amílcar Cabral), Boa Vista (Aristides Pereira), São Vicente (Cesária Évora)
  - Inter-island connections: TACV Cabo Verde Airlines flights between most islands; Brava and Santo Antão accessible only by sea
  - Currency: Cabo Verdean Escudo (CVE); 1 EUR = 110 CVE; euros widely accepted
  - EASE platform for pre-arrival registration (mandatory from 1 July 2026)
  - 3G/4G coverage throughout archipelago; 5G planned
  - Safe, democratically stable country with friendly population

### 2. Island Characters (from official islands page)
- **Sal**: "Small, flat, arid island... white sand beaches, dry tropical climate... perfect destination to relax... exceptional conditions for water sports (fishing, surfing, diving, kitesurfing, windsurfing). Salinas de Pedra Lume, Buracona lava pool, Santa Maria pier, Viveiro Botanical Garden, Gruta do Amor."
- **Santiago**: Largest island, capital Praia. "Rich cultural diversity... Cidade Velha (oldest European city in tropics)... vibrant markets... agricultural products, handicrafts, traditional music."
- **São Vicente**: "Land of Cesária Évora... Mindelo (most cosmopolitan/European city)... lively nights, cultural activity... Baía das Gatas festival, Mindelact theater festival."
- **Santo Antão**: "Steep rocky landscapes, green valleys... impressive trails (Ribeira Grande, Vale do Paúl, Topo da Coroa 1979m)... best grogue (rum-like drink)... lamb stew with beans."
- **Fogo**: Active volcano (highest point in archipelago). "Wine grown in crater... 6-hour hike to summit with guide... fertile land despite water scarcity."
- **Boa Vista**: "Beaches of thick white sand, deep gentle clear sea... Viana Desert... fresh fish, morna music."
- **Maio**: "Quiet, welcoming, almost intact... protected from tourist crowds... turtle spawning (guided only)... Salinas of Porto Inglês, Morrinho Dunes."
- **Brava**: "Most remote, greenest, most flowery... 'Flowers' Island'... natural pools, Eugénio Tavares Trail, goat cheese."
- **São Nicolau**: "Mystical land... Tarrafal medicinal beaches (titanium/iodine)... modje de São Nicolau (goatling stew)."

### 3. UK FCDO Foreign Travel Advice — Cape Verde
- **URL**: https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/cape-verde
- **Entry requirements**: https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/cape-verde/entry-requirements
- **Key facts retrieved**:
  - Passport validity: 6 months after arrival date
  - Visa-free entry for British citizens up to 30 days
  - Mandatory traveller entry form (EASE platform) at least 5 days before arrival
  - Airport security fee (TSA) paid online; from 1 July 2026, double fee at border if not pre-registered
  - Yellow fever certificate required if arriving from transmission-risk country
  - Children ≤17 travelling alone/without parents: letter of authorisation in Portuguese, certified by solicitor/notary
  - GHIC not accepted; travel insurance essential
