# Optional image-credit display update — 24 October 2026

The owner asked that source URLs and quotations stay internal, with optional public credits omitted where the licence permits. Shared detail galleries and the photography-credits page now omit generated, CC0 and public-domain items from visible credits. Attribution-required licences remain credited, and incomplete or unfamiliar rights records fail closed to visible attribution. Existing photo-library provenance and image assignments are unchanged.

The current library contains 273 image records: 242 with visible attribution requirements, 25 CC0/public-domain records, and 6 generated records. The renderer change affects only public credit text and links. Captions and editorial copy remain unchanged.

Files changed: `src/lib/photo-attribution.mjs`, `src/components/Gallery.astro`, `src/pages/photo-credits.astro`.
