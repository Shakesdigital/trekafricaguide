# Stone Town historical walking tour photo enrichment

**Reviewed:** 22 October 2026 (Africa/Kampala)  
**Published activity:** https://trekafricaguide.com/activities/stone-town-historical-walking-tour/  
**Before:** no activity detail gallery  
**Added:** six distinct Stone Town photographs.

The complete published activity record was reviewed. It describes a locally supplied historical walking product with market and historic-site stops, but its current route, exact stops and entry access are not independently confirmed. The six-image gallery therefore illustrates Stone Town streets, market and architecture without claiming that the operator visits any pictured site. Dated scenes carry explicit captions; the House of Wonders image shows renovation as photographed in 2021 and is not presented as its current state. People in the 2009 alley frame are unnamed and are not identified as tour participants. No factual activity copy was changed. The activity hero and gallery use the existing supported fields and gallery renderer (five-image preview with six-image lightbox); image credit links remain in the existing gallery credit details.

## Global deduplication and exclusions

All six distinct Commons source-page URLs and SHA-256 values were compared with every other photo-library image assignment, including the Unguja destination’s six Stone Town/coast photographs, starter images, destination/attraction entries and existing activity mappings. No source URL or file hash is reused. The local public image tree was also hashed for collisions. The unrelated 2023 narrow-street candidate was not selected because it repeated a narrow-lane/door scene already used by Unguja. A separate Darajani shoes-stall candidate (LUCY MBUNGO) was screened but not used; its page describes the market as near Stone Town and gave less direct location evidence than the selected in-town subjects. The House of Wonders frame is kept only as a date-labelled archival renovation view; it is not a current site-condition or tour-access claim.
