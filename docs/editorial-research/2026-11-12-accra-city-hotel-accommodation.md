## Property choice

Accra City Hotel (Barnes Road, downtown Accra) — full-service four-star city hotel, closest full-service fit to the memorial-park/High Street anchor among the candidates checked. Alternatives considered: Labadi Beach Hotel (beachfront, outside the downtown anchor) and Movenpick Ambassador (CBD; no exact OTA source check done this run — Novotel-legacy property was the better-documented fit).
