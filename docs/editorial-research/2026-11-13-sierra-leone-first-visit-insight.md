**Key facts for the insight**:

**Entry & visas** (FCDO + NTB FAQs):
- Passport must have expiry date ≥6 months after visa application date
- Visa required: $80 USD cash on arrival for most nationals; $25 for African Union nationals; e-visa available at https://evisa.sl/#/home (credit/debit card payment)
- Single-entry visa: valid 90 days from issue, max 30 days stay
- Multiple-entry visa: valid 365 days, max 30 days per visit
- Yellow fever vaccination certificate mandatory for entry
- Health travel declaration required within 72 hours before travel via Sierra Leone Health Travel Portal (QR code/reference must be presented)
- Ebola screening measures in place on arrival (WHO PHEIC)

**Airport & transfers** (NTB + country page):
- Lungi International Airport (FNA) — state-of-the-art facilities
- Freetown access: ferry across Sierra Leone River (30–40 min, weather/traffic dependent) or helicopter (10 min)
- Airport security fees: $25 arriving + $25 departing passengers (mandatory)
- Transfers to peninsula properties must be arranged precisely; road approach varies

**Seasonality** (NTB FAQs + country page):
- Dry season: November–April (best for beach/coastal visits)
- Wet season: May–October (torrential downpours, affects movement)
- Harmattan: December–February (dry, dusty, temperatures up to 40°C)
- Coastal temps: 23–30°C

**Freetown Peninsula base decision** (country page + NTB beaches):
- Tokeh Beach: "sparkling white sand, palms in the background, shining turquoise waters", ~20 miles from Freetown near York, fishing/tourism economy, home to The Place Resort ("one of the largest and most attractive beaches in West Africa")
- River Number 2: "white powdery beach fringed by palm trees, where the emerald waters of a river meet the turquoise Atlantic Ocean", 15 km from Freetown, "one of Sierra Leone's most alluring and celebrated attractions", next to Tokeh Beach
- Freetown city: Lumley Beach shoreline, urban base, Hotel Barmoi (Aberdeen, Cape Road, ~400 m from Lumley Beach, 35 km from Lungi airport)

**Activities & experiences** (NTB):
- Tacugama Chimpanzee Sanctuary (40 min from Freetown, Western Area Forest Reserve)
- Bunce Island (ruins reclaimed by vegetation, slavery history)
- Tiwai Island (pygmy hippos, 100+ birds, primates)
- Gola National Park (biodiversity hotspot, Liberia border)
- Bureh Beach (surfers' favourite, 4 km crescent, forest-backed)
- York Beach (culture, history, wildlife + beach)
- EcoFest, birdwatching, hiking/trekking, surfing, cultural experiences

**Practical guidance** (FCDO + NTB):
- Confirm meals, payment, electricity, return transport before arrival
- Use local advice for swimming/boat activities; agree equipment/guiding
- Respect community rules and photography expectations
- Verify current official travel/entry info separately from promotional descriptions
- Clarify shared vs private activities, guiding, meals, conservation charges, transfers in quotations
- Ask about minimum ages and physical requirements for walking activities
- Leave time for rest and unexpected transport changes
- Treat entry rules and official travel advice as separate checks from itinerary
- Cash/ATM guidance: hire driver, avoid night travel
- Export license required for precious stones; verify gem quality

**Money** (NTB FAQs):
- Currency: Sierra Leonean Leone (SLL)
- Costs: accommodation and meal prices vary; FAQ notes expense questions but no fixed figures published
