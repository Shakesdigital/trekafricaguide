## Sources (all checked 2026-11-11)

1. **Visit Rwanda (RDB), Gorilla Tracking** — https://visitrwanda.com/interests/gorilla-tracking/
   Claims used: visitors gather at Volcanoes NP HQ in Kinigi at 7 a.m.; family allocated on the day by fitness levels; briefing on protocols; hikes 30 minutes to 4+ hours; altitude 2,500–4,000 m; twelve fully habituated families; eight permits per troop per day; 96 permits per day; permit cost US $1,500 pp/day; minimum age 15; booking via Irembo (https://irembo.gov.rw/rolportal/web/rdb/mountain-gorilla-tracking) or licensed operators; packing recommendations (solid walking shoes with gaiters, sturdy clothing vs stinging nettles, gardening/leather gloves, lightweight waterproof jacket); porters available.
2. **Visit Rwanda, Volcanoes National Park** — https://www.visitrwanda.com/destinations/volcanoes-national-park/
   Claims used: Virunga Massif context (Karisimbi 4,507 m), park experience framing, "what to wear" guidance. Official RDB booking portal referenced: https://visitrwandabookings.rdb.rw/rdbportal/web/rdb
3. **Rwanda Directorate General of Immigration and Emigration, Visa on arrival** — https://www.migration.gov.rw/visa-on-arrival/
   Claims used: from 1 Jan 2018 citizens of all countries may obtain visa on arrival without prior application; AU/Commonwealth/La Francophonie member citizens get 30-day visit with visa fees waived; online application option via Irembo; fee ceiling US $50 single entry / US $70 multiple entry.
