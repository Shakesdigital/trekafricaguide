# Cape Coast Kakum attraction guide — 5 November 2026

## Scope and editorial decision

Expanded the existing published Cape Coast Kakum attraction (canonical slug `cape-coast-kakum`) in place. No new attraction, activity, country, accommodation or relationship was created. The guide separates Cape Coast Castle and Kakum National Park into distinct visits and recommends a locally based, two-day pace. No booking CTA or unverified property relation was added.

## Evidence checked on 5 November 2026

- Ghana Forestry Commission, [Kakum National Park & Assin](https://fc.gov.gh/kakum-national-park-assin/): conservation area context, forest trails, guided interpretation, canopy walkway.
- Ghana Tourism Authority, [Kakum National Park](https://visitghana.com/kakum-national-park/): indicative road journey of about four hours from Accra, possible public-transport connection from Cape Coast and visitor-centre context. The guide notes traffic and service variability rather than treating the estimate as a promise.
- Ghana Heritage Conservation Trust, [Plan your visit](https://www.kakumpark.com/plan-your-visit/): height exposure, sturdy footwear, humid rainforest conditions and guidance to check current operations.
- Ghana Museums and Monuments Board, [Cape Coast Castle](https://gmmb.gov.gh/cape-coast-castle-cape-coast-1653/): public historical museum and current page hours. Its fees are explicitly marked as reviewed in July 2023, so no prices are reproduced.
- Ghana Tourism Authority, [visitor seasonal guidance](https://visitghana.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Welcome-Ghana-Passport-Brochure-compressed.pdf): broad southern Ghana wet/drier period guidance, presented as seasonal planning context rather than a forecast.
- TripAdvisor, [Kakum National Park visitor reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g303866-d478872-Reviews-Kakum_National_Park-Cape_Coast_Central_Region.html): individual March and September 2026 accounts. One visitor noted some group members stopped due to height and Sunday became busier around midday; another praised guide reassurance. Used as personal experiences, not guarantees.
- TripAdvisor, [Cape Coast Castle visitor reviews](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g303866-d478868-Reviews-Cape_Coast_Castle-Cape_Coast_Central_Region.html): visitor went October 2025, review published 3 February 2026, describing a clear, respectful tour and emotionally heavy history. Used to support reflection time, not as an objective rating.
