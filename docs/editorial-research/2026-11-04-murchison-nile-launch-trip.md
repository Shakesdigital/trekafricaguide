## Research and editorial decision — 4 November 2026

- The canonical attraction is record 59, Murchison Falls National Park. Current published nearby stay data includes Paraa Safari Lodge, whose own guest page describes an approximately three-hour launch trip and says the upper-falls climb takes 45–60 minutes and requires a UWA ranger. The activity copy links the published attraction and stay rather than creating new records.
- UWA's current park page verifies Paraa access context, road estimates and Pakuba Airfield as the most convenient air arrival for the Paraa camp cluster. UWA's park brochure describes the upstream falls route as an approximately three-hour return trip and distinguishes it from the downstream Nile Delta excursion. Its underlying brochure is dated; stable route context only is drawn from it.
- Current Wild Frontiers information confirms an upstream falls cruise from its south-bank Paraa jetty, approximate duration, variable morning/afternoon departures, prearranged pickup/dropoff, and vehicle arrangements for guests who disembark to hike. Paraa Safari Lodge publishes a different normal schedule. The copy therefore asks travelers to confirm the specific operator, jetty and schedule instead of generalizing one provider's times.
- Tripadvisor's Waterfall Cruises listing displayed dated accounts: KTP310 (10 Oct 2023) described a three-hour UWA launch, wildlife stops and a distant falls view; Damien G (11 Oct 2025) reported an early return during rain concerns and litter on the bank. The guide attributes these as personal accounts, not guarantees, and uses the complaint to advise a weather contingency and taking litter back.
- No exact current OTA product for this specific upstream launch trip was verified. No booking CTA or destination was added. A neutral image state is intentional; the six-photo subject list is recorded in progress.json.
