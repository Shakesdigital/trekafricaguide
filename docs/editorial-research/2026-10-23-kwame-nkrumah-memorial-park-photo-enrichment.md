## Collection and rights

Six visually reviewed photographs from Wikimedia Commons depict the exact Kwame Nkrumah Memorial Park in Accra. The two wide daytime views and four night views were captured in October 2023. Source pages identify the site by name; three files also provide GPS coordinates within the memorial grounds. The photographs show architecture, fountains and sculpture; they do not depict or identify a tour operator, guide or activity participant. Two incidental visitors appear in the central-walkway image and are not identified or presented as participants. No room, service or current event is implied.
