- The four selected photos are exact to Livingstone, Mosi-oa-Tunya or the local Zambezi setting. The Pexels results did not produce usable town, museum or craft photographs with sufficiently precise subject/permission evidence.
- A second Pexels falls result had only broad Zambia location support and was visually redundant. A Commons search surfaced attribution-required material, which is not eligible under the current no-public-attribution instruction. Both were omitted.
- Photographs of museum collections need explicit reuse permission; photographs of craft makers or identifiable people need creator/subject consent. Neither was established, so neither category was used.
- Global source-page URL and SHA-256 checks found no duplicate among the existing image library or public image files. Existing mandatory-credit images elsewhere were left unchanged.
