## Facts and sources (all checked 7 November 2026)
1. Visit Rwanda — Nyungwe National Park destination page: "One of the oldest rainforests in Africa"; biodiversity; a small population of chimpanzees plus 12 other primate species, including the Albertine-Rift-endemic L'Hoest's monkey. https://visitrwanda.com/destinations/nyungwe-national-park-2/ (page partially erroring downstream; core text retrieved via r.jina.ai render).
2. Visit Rwanda — Canopy Walkway interest page: 160 m long and 70 m high suspension bridge above a ravine in Nyungwe; accessed as part of a guided tour along the Igishigishigi trail, or when hiking the Imbaraga or Umuyove trails; about an hour's walk to the walkway from the Uwinka Reception Centre; butterflies, orchids and endemic birds; 13 primate species (chance encounters); well-maintained footpaths that become muddy after rain; listed cost categories (Rwandan/EAC citizen USD 5; foreign resident in Rwanda/EAC USD 20; international visitor USD 40); open all year with more chance of rain in the afternoons; boots and raincoat rental plus walking stick available from park headquarters; "what to wear" suitable footwear and lightweight raincoat. https://visitrwanda.com/interests/canopy-walkway/
3. Visit Rwanda — Primate Tracking interest page (checked 6–7 November 2026): chimpanzees in Nyungwe; tracking starts early at 5:30 am and can last an hour to several hours; maximum one hour with the chimps once found; permits bookable online (Irembo), at an RDB office or via a tour operator; steep, slippery, muddy terrain and thick vegetation; suitable footwear and fitness essential.
4. Visit Rwanda — Weather page: rainy seasons mid-February–May and mid-September–mid-December; dry season June–mid-September with easier roads and paths. https://visitrwanda.com/facts/weather/
5. Cost handling: the canopy-walkway "Cost" box on Visit Rwanda (USD 5 / 20 / 40 by category) is published on the page and is quoted with a reconfirm caveat; chimpanzee-tracking permit fees are not quoted because no retrievable current official figure page was verified this run — readers are directed to the official booking channel.
