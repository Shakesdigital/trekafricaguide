## Key Facts (from official website, checked 2026-11-12)

**History & Positioning:**
- Established 1959 — "Botswana's finest heritage stay", decades of hosting adventurers in Kasane
- Riverfront location on the Chobe River, neighbouring Chobe National Park
- "Best expedition base for Chobe River boat cruises, day trips to Victoria Falls and more"
- Closest boat cruise departure point in Kasane

**Access:**
- 10-minute drive from Chobe National Park entrance gate (Sedudu gate)
- 10-minute drive from Kasane International Airport (BBK)
- Victoria Falls (VFA) 90-minute road transfer via Kazungula Bridge

**Accommodation Categories (from official accommodation page):**
1. **Safari Rooms** — elegant river-facing rooms, modern amenities, Victorian ball-and-claw bubble bath
2. **River Rooms** — accommodation wing closest to main lodge complex, faces secluded indigenous riverside garden
3. **Bush Rooms** — at Chobe Bush Lodge (boutique retreat within walking distance of Chobe Safari Lodge), fewer rooms
4. **Classic Rondavels** — lovingly restored to honour heritage status while providing modern amenities
5. **Riverfront Campsite** — best sunset view in Kasane, camp beside Chobe bushbuck

**Room Features (all rooms):**
- Wi-Fi, satellite TV, mosquito covering, mini-fridge, air-conditioning, balcony

**Activities & Experiences (from official website):**
- Boat cruises on Chobe River — "Africa's best boat safari", up-close wildlife, reliable game viewing
- Game drives in Chobe National Park
- Day trips to Victoria Falls (90 minutes via Kazungula Bridge)
- Tiger fishing
- Village tours
- Scenic flights
- Spa treatments
- Sedudu Bar and Grill (dining on the deck)

**Dining:**
- Sedudu Bar and Grill — lunch beside the Chobe River, dinner on the deck, sunset cocktails
