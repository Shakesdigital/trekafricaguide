**Key Facts for Travel Insight:**

- **Entry:** E-visa required (apply ≥4 working days before arrival via evisa.bj); yellow fever certificate mandatory; passport valid for stay duration. FCDO (British passport scope): no visa needed for up to 90 days — **contradiction**; FCDO says "contact Beninese Embassy in France" but Benin gov portal says e-visa mandatory for most. Clarify: check own nationality.

- **Regional safety:** Southern Benin (Atlantique department: Cotonou, Ouidah, Ganvié, Porto-Novo) NOT under FCDO advisory. Northern parks (Parc du W, Pendjari) and border zones: do not travel / essential only. This insight covers southern route only.

- **Ouidah:** Slave trade heritage (Door of No Return, Route des Esclaves, Portuguese Fort/Ouidah Museum of History); Vodun heartland (Python Temple, Sacred Forest of Kpasse, annual Vodun festival 10 Jan); basilica; ~40 km from Cotonou (~1 hour by road).

- **Ganvié:** Stilt village on Lake Nokoué; ~20,000 residents (Tofinu); boat-only access; fishing culture; UNESCO tentative list; ~1 hour from Cotonou by road + boat; guided pirogue tours standard.

- **Base choice:** Cotonou (largest city, airport, most accommodation, transport hub) vs Ouidah (heritage immersion, quieter, beach access) vs Ganvié (overnight in stilt village — limited, basic). Most first-time visitors base in Cotonou or Ouidah.

- **Route logic:** Country guide recommends "each place its own outing rather than trying to compress both into a single day." Minimum 2 days for southern route: Day 1 Ouidah (slave route, Vodun sites, museum), Day 2 Ganvié (pirogue tour, village life). Add Porto-Novo (capital, Afro-Brazilian mosque, royal palaces) if time allows.

- **Transport:** Cotonou Cadjehoun Airport (COO) main entry. Road: Cotonou–Ouidah ~40 km, ~1 hr; Cotonou–Ganvié departure point ~30 min, then 30–45 min pirogue. Arrange boat for Ganvié explicitly (lifejackets, guide, return plan). Do not assume lake visit can be added casually.

- **Cultural etiquette:** Vodun ceremonies not blanket invitation to photograph/enter; ask permission before photographing people, homes, religious activity; guides should explain who hosts cultural experiences and what visitors are welcome to do. Sacred Forest and Python Temple have specific rules.
