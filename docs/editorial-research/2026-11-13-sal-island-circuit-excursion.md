**Key facts for the guide** (all attributed):
- The circuit is a full-day road outing (~7 h per the verified listing), flat island, distinct stops not one continuous site (attraction guide's own framing).
- Pedra de Lume: salt works in a volcanic crater on the NE side; national heritage (IPC). Float/bathing only when offered; confirm access and conditions locally; protect eyes/skin from concentrated salt water.
- Buracona Blue Eye: light effect in a coastal lava cave; appearance depends on sun angle/conditions; midday timing commonly aimed for; walk only on marked viewing areas; no swim-safety guarantee (a 11 Nov 2026 GYG reviewer reported swimming was not possible due to sea conditions that day).
- Shark Bay: shallow-water lemon sharks (optional stop in the verified listing); wildlife viewing at respectful distance; one 11 Nov 2026 reviewer found the stop short.
- Kite Beach stop operates October–March only (operator-listed).
- Entrance fees €5 (Blue Eye) / €6 (Salinas) and optional lunch €11/€6 are operator-listed figures on the product page — carry with "operator-listed, reconfirm" hedge.
- Best time: official national guidance Nov–Jul dry / Aug–Oct hotter and occasionally rainy (turismo.cv); sea/wind conditions assessed locally; no month guarantee.
