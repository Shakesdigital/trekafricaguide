## Key facts to include

- **Altitude/location**: Kasane sits at ~950 m on the Chobe River, Botswana's northeastern corner, bordering Namibia (Caprivi Strip), Zambia, and Zimbabwe (Victoria Falls).
- **Gateway role**: Kasane (BBK) has scheduled flights from Johannesburg, Gaborone, and Maun. Victoria Falls (VFA) is a 90-minute road transfer (adds the waterfall as a bonus day).
- **Chobe Riverfront**: Dry-season (May–Oct) elephant concentrations — herds of hundreds at the river, visible from boat cruises and game drives based in Kasane. Most accessible part of Chobe NP.
- **Four ecosystems**: Riverfront (Kasane base), Savuti Marsh (predator densities, seasonal zebra migration), Linyanti Swamps (remote, fly-in camps), Nogatsaa (dry interior, seasonal).
- **Accommodation range**: Houseboats on the Chobe River, riverbank lodges, fly-in camps for Savuti/Linyanti/Nogatsaa.
- **Activities**: Boat cruises (morning/late afternoon), game drives in riverfront zone. Savuti/Linyanti/Nogatsaa require fly-in camps.
- **Combination logic**: Classic two-area Botswana safari = Chobe Riverfront (2–3 nights) + Okavango camp (3–4 nights). Connecting flight Kasane (BBK) → Maun (MUB) = 45 min by light aircraft.
- **Green season (Nov–Apr)**: Chobe disperses, riverfront quieter, birding exceptional. Okavango recedes, some mokoro routes shorten.
- **Entry requirements**: Visa-free for many (30–90 days), passport 6 months, yellow fever cert if from endemic country. Confirm for own passport.
- **Baggage**: Light aircraft limit typically 20 kg soft bag.
- **Cross-border**: Victoria Falls (Zimbabwe) and Livingstone (Zambia) accessible from Kasane — separate entry formalities.
