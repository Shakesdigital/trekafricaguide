# Cape Coast & Kakum attraction photo collection — 22 October 2026

Guide: [Cape Coast & Kakum](https://trekafricaguide.com/attractions/cape-coast-kakum/). The existing photo collection had two generic stock images. The article connects Kakum's forest/canopy experience with Cape Coast's sensitive historical sites; the six selected images show five distinct Kakum forest/canopy contexts and one Cape Coast Castle exterior. Castle views are descriptive, not celebratory; no guide, guest or current operation is inferred. No factual copy was changed.

All selected images were opened and visually inspected. Exact subject, creator, license and capture date were checked on each original Commons file page on 22 October 2026. The files were downloaded at up to 1,280 pixels wide via Commons' file redirect and resized only; the CC BY-SA images remain under their respective ShareAlike terms. Source pages are distinct from other detail-page collections; local SHA-256 hashes were compared with the public image library and all local public image assets. No selected source URL or byte-identical file was already assigned elsewhere.
