# Victoria Falls Zambezi rafting photo enrichment

Checked 20 October 2026 (Africa/Kampala). Activity: [Victoria Falls Zambezi white-water rafting](https://trekafricaguide.com/activities/victoria-falls-zambezi-white-water-rafting/). The guide describes a Wild Horizons rafting day on the Zambezi through Batoka Gorge, Zimbabwe. These images illustrate the river setting and white-water rafting; none is represented as a Wild Horizons departure or as the guide’s actual participants.

All six candidates were inspected visually. The five rafting frames are distinct moments (rapid entry, active white water, raft lifted by a rapid, regrouping, and group on the river); the sixth is a wider gorge/river landscape. Exact Commons file pages and current revisions were checked on 20 October 2026.
