## Sources (all checked 2026-11-17)

### 1. Official Maasai Mara Conservancies vs Reserve — maasaimara.ke
- URL: https://maasaimara.ke/conservancies/conservancy-vs-national-reserve/
- 2026 fees and rules from the official Mara conservancies site
- Key facts: Reserve = government-run, standard daytime game drives on established tracks; Conservancies = Maasai-owned land leased to tourism partners, off-road driving, night drives, walking safaris, lower vehicle density
- Mara Triangle treated as part of National Reserve
- Conservancy stays can complement Reserve safari when access and charges are clear

### 2. Africa Geographic — Mara Conservancy or Reserve Guide
- URL: https://travel.africageographic.com/destinations/kenya/maasai-mara/mara-conservancy-or-reserve-guide
- Reserve: direct access to Mara River crossings during Great Migration, Musiara Marsh plains, cost-effective, activities limited to standard daytime game drives on established tracks
- Conservancies: controlled environment, exclusivity, off-road, night drives, walking, lower crowds
- Same wildlife, same horizon — different rules, crowds, freedoms

### 3. GoKenyaSafari — Conservancies vs Reserve Explained (2026)
- URL: https://gokenyasafari.com/maasai-mara-conservancies-vs-the-reserve-the-difference-explained/
- Conservancy = block of Maasai-owned land next to reserve, pooled parcels leased to small number of tourism partners
- Real difference sits in accommodation, not entry
- Vehicle limits: Reserve no limit; conservancies typically 2-3 vehicles per sighting
- Night drives and walking only in conservancies

### 4. Maasai Mara Reserve vs Conservancies — masaimarasafaripackages.net
- URL: https://masaimarasafaripackages.net/guides/reserve-vs-conservancies/
- Detailed breakdown: wildlife rules, off-road driving, fees, accommodation
- Migration access: Reserve has direct river crossing access; conservancies border the reserve

### 5. The African Traveler — Conservancies vs National Reserve
- URL: https://theafricantraveler.com/maasai-mara-conservancies-vs-national-reserve/
- Compare by crowds, cost, activities, migration access, wildlife viewing, safari style
