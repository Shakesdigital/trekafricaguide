## Claim checks

- The Egyptian Ministry of Tourism and Antiquities describes the Giza plateau as a wider archaeological landscape of pyramids, tombs, temples and causeways. The article uses this to encourage a selective route, not to assert which exact monuments are currently accessible: https://egymonuments.gov.eg/en/archaeological-sites/giza-plateau/
- The Egyptian Monuments ticket portal lists plateau ticket categories, schedule/last-entry information and advises arriving at least an hour before closing. The Ministry overview and ticket portal show different hours, so the article avoids stating a definitive schedule, advises a margin and directs readers to recheck the exact visit date: https://egymonuments.com/locations/details/Pyramids
- GEM's official visit and ticketing sites provide current visit planning, location, ticket and schedule information. The guide advises checking the selected date and using the official channel rather than reproducing a possibly changeable schedule: https://gem.eg/visit/plan-your-visit and https://tickets.gem.eg/rvmp
- Cairo Governorate's metro information (last updated 2026) identifies Giza station, says it provides access to buses heading to the pyramids, and describes the Grand Egyptian Museum metro route as under construction. The article explicitly avoids promising direct GEM metro access: https://www.cairo.gov.eg/en/governorate-services/transportation-services/metro/
- Egypt's e-Visa portal and FAQ make clear that eligibility depends on nationality; tourism and other purposes are not interchangeable. Experience Egypt is linked as a further official tourism source. The guide makes no universal visa or transit claim and directs non-tourism/complex cases to the relevant embassy/consulate and carrier: https://www.visa2egypt.gov.eg/ ; https://www.visa2egypt.gov.eg/eVisa/FAQ ; https://www.experienceegypt.eg/en/usefulinfo
