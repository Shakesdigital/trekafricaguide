**Key facts for the activity guide**:

**What the excursion covers** (from attraction/destination/NTB sources):
- River Number 2 estuary: "where the emerald waters of a river meet the turquoise Atlantic Ocean" (Ministry of Tourism), 15 km from Freetown
- Tokeh Beach: "sparkling white sand, palms in the background, shining turquoise waters" (Ministry of Tourism), ~20 miles from Freetown near York
- Community-based outings: NTB describes "peninsula beach activities list"; attraction guide advises "agree the price, equipment and return time in advance" for "community visit or boat outing"
- Tide and weather awareness: attraction guide warns "Do not assume that a beach-to-beach walk or crossing is appropriate under every tide or weather condition. Ask about the day's conditions and use transport when that is the sensible option"
- Transfer planning: "Arrange a road transfer from Freetown to the exact property or beach entrance. Ask for current road conditions and the best route rather than relying on an older travel account"
- Swimming caution: "A clear day does not establish that every stretch is safe for swimming" and "avoid entering river mouths or unfamiliar water on appearance alone"

**Extended peninsula context** (NTB + country page):
- Tacugama Chimpanzee Sanctuary: 40 min from Freetown, Western Area Forest Reserve
- Bunce Island: ruins reclaimed by vegetation, slavery history
- Bureh Beach: surfers' favourite, 4 km crescent, forest-backed
- York Beach: culture, history, wildlife + beach
- EcoFest, birdwatching, hiking/trekking, surfing, cultural experiences listed by NTB

**Seasonality** (NTB, checked 21 Nov 2026):
- Dry season: November–April (best for beach/coastal visits)
- Wet season: May–October (torrential downpours, affects movement)
- Harmattan: December–February (dry, dusty, temperatures up to 40°C)
- Coastal temps: 23–30°C
