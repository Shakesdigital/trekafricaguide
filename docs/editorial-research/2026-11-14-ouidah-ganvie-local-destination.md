**Key facts for the guide:**
- **Geography:** Ouidah (coastal town, Atlantic) and Ganvié (stilt village on Lake Nokoué) are ~20 km apart. Cotonou (airport, main city) is ~40 km east of Ouidah.
- **Heritage:** Ouidah = Route des Esclaves (slave route), Door of No Return, Temple of Pythons, Sacred Forest of Kpassè, Zinsou Foundation, Maison du Brésil. Ganvié = Lake Nokoué stilt village, fishing, floating market, water-based livelihoods.
- **Vodun:** Living tradition in both places; respectful access and photography permissions essential.
- **Access:** Fly into Cotonou (COO). Arrange road transfer to Ouidah/Ganvié base. Ganvié requires boat from designated embarkation point (lifejackets mandatory per official info). Do not treat boat visit as casual add-on.
- **Best time:** Discuss with local provider; weather/water conditions matter for Ganvié boat; ceremonies/events may affect Ouidah access. No blanket festival invitation.
- **Planning:** Agree guiding, transport, admission costs before departure. Ask permission before photographing people/homes/religious activity. Check regional travel advice beyond southern route. Choose accommodation by actual road/boat access.
- **Travel advisory context:** Southern Benin (Ouidah/Ganvié/Cotonou) is NOT under FCDO advisory; northern/eastern regions are. Frame honestly.
