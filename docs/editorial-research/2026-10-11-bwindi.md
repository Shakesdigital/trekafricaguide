## Verified starting evidence

Uganda Wildlife Authority's [gorilla tracking page](https://ugandawildlife.org/activities/gorilla-tracking/) lists Buhoma, Ruhija, Rushaga and Nkuringo as Bwindi's four main trekking sectors. It describes trek duration as approximately two to seven hours and the observation period with habituated gorillas as one hour. Present duration as variable, not guaranteed; distinguish standard tracking from other products. Practical editorial implication: plan accommodation and arrival around the booked sector, not a generic 'near Bwindi' label.

Use the authority's [July 2024 tracking guidelines](https://ugandawildlife.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Guidelines-for-the-management-of-gorilla-and-chimpanzee-tracking-JULY-2024.pdf) as a document to check against current guidance, not proof that all rules and prices remain current. Do not publish permit fees until the effective tariff has been verified.
