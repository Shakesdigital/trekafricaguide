## Access and permits

- MEFT permit FAQ: https://www.meft.gov.na/frequently-asked-questions/what-are-the-entry-permit-requirements-for-the-various-parks-in-namibia-/136/ — park permits are issued at gates; Sesriem office issues Namib-Naukluft permits only; NWR does not issue permits. Also says visitors must stay on indicated roads.
- MEFT December 2025 concession notice: https://www.meft.gov.na/news/299/Clarification-on-the-Shuttle-Concession-for-Sossusvlei--Namib-Naukluft-Park/ — background, not current after 2026 changes.
- The MEFT 14 April notice was reported as restricting tourist self-drive beyond 2x4 parking effective 1 May. Concession archive: https://sossusvlei.aboutafrica.co/media-statements . This was quickly reversed.
- Informanté on MEFT's 2 May public clarification: https://informante.web.na/?p=394001 — reports 4x4 self-drivers and registered guides may continue, shuttle available, buses/trucks prohibited. The official MEFT archive did not surface the May 2 notice during this check. Therefore explain the reversal with attributed reporting and make a fresh same-week check explicit; do not state the rule as guaranteed for a future trip.
- NWR Sesriem official: https://www.nwr.com.na/resorts/sesriem-campsite/ — identifies Sesriem campsite by canyon/gateway, Sossusvlei 60 km, shuttle listed from 2x4 parking. Availability/terms/rates can change; no fee quoted.
