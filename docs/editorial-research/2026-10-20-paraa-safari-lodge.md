## Traveler perspective

The page summarizes a small number of dated Tripadvisor accounts rather than repeating ratings or review totals. One family’s August 2026 review praised the comfort of a modern room and staff help with dietary needs, while noting bathroom splash from a shower without a screen. A July 2026 reviewer described differences between refurbished and older rooms. A late-August reviewer reported unavailable shared facilities; management's response said refurbishment was phased and the pool was being replaced. These opposing reports support direct pre-arrival questions; they do not establish the current condition of every room or facility.
