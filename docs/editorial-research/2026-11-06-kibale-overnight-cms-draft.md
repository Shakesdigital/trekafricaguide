<h2>Fort Portal or the forest for the night before your trek?</h2>
<p>Start with the reporting point on your booking. For an early walk from Kanyanchu Visitor Centre, a forest-side stay can make the morning easier. Ask your host how long the drive from their door to the centre usually takes and whether breakfast can be ready before pickup. A lodge address that says “Kibale” tells you less about your morning than those two answers.</p>
<p>Choose <a href="/countries/uganda/fort-portal/">Fort Portal</a> if the town itself is part of the trip. You might spend the afternoon at Mpanga Market and stay in town for dinner, then head to the forest the next morning. Work backwards from registration and the ranger briefing when agreeing a pickup with your driver. Uganda Wildlife Authority puts the park about an hour from Fort Portal. Your hotel, the road and the entrance you need all affect that estimate. If you are travelling solo, price the outward and return journeys together before comparing a town room with a forest lodge.</p>
<p>With two nights to allocate, an early permit favours sleeping near Kanyanchu first and moving into town after the walk. Agree where your bags will stay and how to contact the driver when you return. If your main pleasure is an unhurried evening after the trek, both nights in the same room spare you a checkout on trekking day.</p>
