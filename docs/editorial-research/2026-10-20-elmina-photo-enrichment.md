# Elmina local guide photo enrichment

Checked 20 October 2026. Published destination: https://trekafricaguide.com/countries/ghana/elmina/

## Scope

Six distinct licensed photographs support the guide’s account of Elmina as a living coastal town with fishing communities and heritage context. The set covers town views, streets, harbor and Benya Lagoon. All six selected files were visually inspected. Exact Commons source pages and original source SHA-1s were compared with every photo-library record; local SHA-256 values were checked against all existing recorded hashes. No source page or image hash is reused. Castle attraction images were excluded.
