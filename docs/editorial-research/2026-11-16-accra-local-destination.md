## Gap and reader impact

The live Ghana country page (https://trekafricaguide.com/countries/ghana/, HTTP 200, checked 16 November 2026) carries a "Destinations in Ghana" rail with a single entry, Elmina (https://trekafricaguide.com/countries/ghana/elmina, HTTP 200). Ghana already has published guides that sit inside the capital: the Kwame Nkrumah Memorial Park attraction and its guided-visit activity, and the Accra City Hotel stay. The country page and the Ghana first-trip insight both frame Accra as the arrival point and the base before the castle coast, but there is no Accra destination page for either to link to, and no page where a reader can plan an Accra arrival day, a city day and an onward transfer.

Reader impact: Accra is where almost every Ghana itinerary begins, through Kotoka International Airport. Without a city guide, readers have to infer from a hotel page and an insight how to spend an arrival day, how the historic districts relate to one another, when to attempt the coast, and what the published memorial-park hours and fees are.

The new page fills that gap without duplicating the attraction guide: the Kwame Nkrumah Memorial Park record covers the site itself; this page covers the city around it (districts, arrival, pacing, onward transfers) and links out to the attraction, the activity and the stay.

## Editorial position

The record is a city guide, not a site guide. It states the city's structure (capital since 1877; Greater Accra's two metropolitan areas; the historic districts and the coastal edge), then organises the visit around districts and separate outings rather than a checklist. It repeats no attraction copy: the memorial park's own record and guided-visit activity carry the site detail, and this page links to both.

Facts are limited to what the official sources above state on the checked date. Prices and hours for the Kwame Nkrumah Memorial Park are quoted as published with the check date and a prompt to reconfirm, because the park's own page marks them as current rates rather than a tariff in force. No road journey times, taxi fares, hotel rates, or month-by-month rainfall figures were invented. The climate section uses the tourism authority's own characterisation (warm and tropical; Accra in the dry equatorial zone) and otherwise advises checking a current forecast. No traveller review is quoted because no attributable dated review of a specific Accra service was verified this run, so no review field is populated.
