## Sources checked
1. **Zimbabwe Tourism Authority (zimbabwetourism.net) Victoria Falls destination page** — checked 13 Nov 2026, HTTP 200: Victoria Falls known as the "Smoke that thunders" (Mosi-oa-Tunya, Tonga); "the largest single curtain of falling water in the world and 70% of the exquisite views are seen from the Zimbabwe rain forest"; the rain forest has "constant rain 24/7 from the never ending spray of the Falls" and a unique ecosystem — "a botanists dream and bird lovers' paradise"; Livingstone first saw the Falls in 1855; "Walking within the Falls is a couple of kilometres and it does get very hot"; town/resort activity range (bungee, white water rafting, abseiling, luxurious river boat cruises); area "has not become over commercialized".
2. **UK FCDO Zimbabwe travel advice** — checked 13 Nov 2026 ("Still current at: 13 November 2026"; updated 15 July 2026):
   - Passport: expiry at least 6 months after arrival, 3 blank pages.
   - Visa: required; most visitors get visa on arrival, usually 30 days; take enough US dollars in small notes; e-visa available at evisa.gov.zw (single or double entry).
   - **KAZA Univisa**: valid for travel between Zimbabwe and Zambia and for day trips into Botswana; available at Harare, Bulawayo and Victoria Falls airports and at the land borders at Victoria Falls (Zimbabwe-Zambia) and Kazungula (Zimbabwe-Botswana); costs US$50; valid 30 days.
   - Yellow fever certificate required when arriving from a transmission-risk country.
   - Cash: declare US$10,000+ brought in; illegal to leave Zimbabwe with cash worth more than US$2,000.
   - Journalists must be accredited before travel.
3. **Live Trek Africa Guide pages re-read 13 Nov 2026 for continuity**: Zimbabwe country page; Victoria Falls attraction guide (park access via Victoria Falls International Airport and the A8 road approach per the park authority; rainforest sustained by spray; activity terms; cross-border passport checks); The Victoria Falls Hotel stay guide (Edwardian landmark established 1904, gardens and views toward the bridge, Stables Signature Wing facilities, no verified booking link yet); guided-walk and rafting activity guides; `plan-your-victoria-falls-visit` insight.
