# Sine-Saloum Delta attraction image collection — 20 October 2026

## Scope and page review

Page: https://trekafricaguide.com/attractions/sine-saloum-delta/

The published guide describes the Sine-Saloum Delta as a broad, inhabited estuary, with mangroves, bolongs, river routes and varied launch points. Its image choices need to support those exact landscape and planning claims without suggesting that sensitive shell mounds or burial places can be landed on, that every shoreline is open to visitors, or that wildlife sightings are guaranteed. The existing guide had one image, but its nesting-site stock hero was also assigned to the Senegal country-detail collection. It is replaced here by six new, distinct images that are not assigned elsewhere. No factual copy changed.

## Rejected candidates

- `Delta du Saloum Sénégal, Février 2023.jpg` was visually inspected but not used because the Commons thumbnail was inverted and showed a restaurant terrace; correcting it would require transformation and could imply an unverified lodge/property scene.
- A Copernicus Sentinel image was not used because it is a satellite image rather than ground-level travel photography.
- No shellfish-harvesting or community-work image was selected; the guide calls for consent and appropriate context before photographing hosted visits.
