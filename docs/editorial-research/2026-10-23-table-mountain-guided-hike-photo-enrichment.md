## Page and caption review

Reviewed the full published activity record and the linked planning context. The guide describes a guided walk focused on mountain ecology, asks travelers to agree the actual route and return arrangements with their provider, and does not say that the linked tour uses Platteklip Gorge or Skeleton Gorge. The images are therefore presented as dated examples of Table Mountain hiking, not as scenes from the listed operator or participants in its activity. No factual prose, design, home/about page, tracking, stay IDs, redirects or canonical settings were changed.

## Photographs

All six images were fetched as 1280-pixel Wikimedia thumbnails, visually inspected, and checked against existing library source URLs and every local public image hash. Their source pages identify the exact mountain/trail; no source URL or hash duplicate was found. Thumbnails were not otherwise edited. Trail and view conditions are archival and do not replace current SANParks or operator guidance.
